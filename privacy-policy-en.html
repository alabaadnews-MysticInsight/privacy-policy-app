Privacy Policy for Dream Interpretation / Mystic Insight

Dream Interpretation / Mystic Insight ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our application for dream interpretation, palmistry, and tasseography.

By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We collect information solely to provide the core functionality of the App. We do not create user accounts or profiles. The information you provide is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers after your session ends.

A. User-Provided Content

  • Dream Descriptions (Text): When you use the dream interpretation feature, you voluntarily provide text describing your dream.
  • Hand Images (for Palmistry): When you use the palmistry feature, you may voluntarily upload a photograph of your hand.
  • Cup/Leaf Images (for Tasseography): When you use the tasseography feature, you may voluntarily upload a photograph of your tea leaves or coffee cup.

Purpose of Use: This user-provided content (text and images) is used solely and exclusively to generate an interpretation or reading for you within the App.

B. Technical Information

Like most apps, we may collect non-personal, anonymized technical data such as crash logs and usage diagnostics through third-party services (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics) to help us improve the App's stability and performance. This data cannot be used to identify you.

2. Third-Party Service Providers (Google Gemini AI)

For premium users, interpretations and readings are powered by Google's Gemini artificial intelligence service.

Data Sharing with Gemini: When you use the AI interpretation feature, the text or images you provide are sent securely to Google's servers to generate a response. This data is processed according to Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AI Data Usage Policies.

We do not control Google's data processing practices. We encourage you to review their policies to understand how they handle data:
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Retention and Your Control

Our "No-Storage" Policy: We are designed with a fundamental "no-storage" principle for non-premium users.

  • User Content: Your dream text, hand images, and cup/leaf images are not stored on our servers after the interpretation is complete. They are processed in memory for the duration of the request and then discarded.
  • Interpretation History: Free users cannot save interpretation history. Only premium users can store interpretations locally on their device.

Premium User Features:

  • Premium users can save up to 10 interpretations (dreams, palmistry readings, or tasseography readings)
  • When the 10-dream limit is reached, the oldest interpretation is automatically deleted to make space for new ones
  • All interpretations are stored locally on your device using secure encryption

Your Rights: You have full and immediate control over your data.

In-App Data Management

Delete Individual Interpretations:

Premium users can delete saved interpretations from "My Dreams" section:

  1. Go to the "My Dreams" section in the app
  2. Find the interpretation you want to delete
  3. Tap the delete button (trash can icon)
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted
  5. The interpretation will be permanently removed from your device

Delete Your Account:

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Go to the Settings page in the app
  2. Tap on "Delete Account"
  3. Confirm your decision when prompted
  4. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted

Delete Input Content:

You can delete your input (text and images) directly within the app:

  1. Navigate to the input field or image display where your content appears
  2. Tap the "Delete" or "Clear" button (typically represented by a trash can icon)
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Note: Since we do not store your content on our servers, deletion from your device or account deletion is permanent and immediate.

4. Biometric Data (Hand Images)

We acknowledge that a photograph of your hand may be considered Biometric Data in some jurisdictions. We want to be perfectly clear:

  • We collect hand images only with your explicit, voluntary action for the purpose of providing a palmistry reading.
  • We do not use these images to identify you or for any purpose other than the real-time analysis by the AI.
  • We do not store, share, or sell your hand images.
